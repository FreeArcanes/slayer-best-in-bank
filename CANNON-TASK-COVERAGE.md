# Cannon Task Coverage

Embedded cannon-route catalog for the current **Slayer Best in Bank** release candidate.

This catalog was audited against current Old School RuneScape Wiki Slayer task/location guidance on 2026-07-25. A task is included when it has a practical Slayer route where a dwarf multicannon can be used. **Cannonability is location-sensitive**: when RuneLite supplies a location-locked task, Best-in-Bank suppresses the cannon method if that assigned area is prohibited or does not match the verified route.

**Catalog routes:** 81

## Important location-sensitive examples

- **Smoke devils:** Smoke Devil Dungeon — cannon lure + Burst/Barrage.
- **Dagannoth:** Lighthouse, Island of Stone/Jormungand's Prison, and Waterbirth Island Dungeon are supported; Catacombs of Kourend is not.
- **Bloodveld:** Meiyerditch Laboratory, Stronghold Slayer Cave, and Iorwerth Dungeon; Catacombs is not.
- **Dust devils:** Smoke Dungeon cannon-permitted area or Wilderness Slayer Cave; Catacombs is not.
- **Metal dragons:** Brimhaven, Isle of Souls (iron), or Lithkren Vault (adamant/rune); Ancient Cavern mithril dragons and Catacombs are not cannon routes.
- **Waterfiends:** Iorwerth Dungeon only; Ancient Cavern and Kraken Cove are not.
- **Wyrms:** Neypotzli Wyrmlings route; Karuulm Wyrms are not cannonable.
- **Shades:** Sepulchre of Death / Mort'ton-outskirts route; Shade Catacombs and Catacombs of Kourend are excluded.

## Cannon gear behavior

When a cannon strategy is selected, Best-in-Bank treats the complete dwarf multicannon as part of the Tier 1 loadout:

- Cannon base
- Cannon stand
- Cannon barrels
- Cannon furnace
- Steel or granite cannonballs remain a required trip supply

Ornamented cannon components are handled as a complete separate set; ornamented and regular parts are never mixed in one recommendation.

## Conservative behavior

The catalog intentionally prefers a known-valid route over assuming a cannon works everywhere a monster appears. Tasks without a verified cannon route continue to use their normal Best-in-Bank strategy instead of receiving a speculative cannon option.
